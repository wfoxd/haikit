Remove all, at the end of the dock's shelf, puts every docked surface back in the chat at once: the open one and those in the pile too, each in its own card, still mounted. The window and the pile's list close with the shelf, and focus moves to the message box. It's an icon, labelled `Remove all from the dock` for screen readers and named in a tooltip, and the shelf's arrow keys reach it.
